Where It Started

Kampala, and one question at the door

Retro Wave began as a Kampala nightlife night built around one question: what is the anthem that made you? Every set since has been a mix of those answers — decade-spanning, unapologetically nostalgic, and loud in all the right places.

Three editions in, the format has not changed. What has changed is the reach — Retro Wave is taking the same night to Nairobi and Kigali, with one platform handling tickets, history and discovery across all three.

Questions

Good to know

Where are events held? Uganda first — Kampala, Entebbe and Jinja — with Nairobi and Kigali dates opening as they are confirmed. Every date is listed on the Events page.
How do I pay? MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or Visa and Mastercard. You approve mobile money on your own handset — there is nothing to install.
Can I bring someone else's ticket? Tickets can be reassigned to a friend from your ticket link, no new purchase needed. Each QR is single-use, so a screenshot of someone else's ticket will not scan twice.
Is there an age limit? Retro Wave nights are 18+ and valid ID is required at the door.
Is the website hard to use on a small phone? It should not be. There is a text-size control in the menu — tap the menu button, then A, A or A+ — and the whole site gets bigger and stays that way on every page.
