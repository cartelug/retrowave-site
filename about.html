Our Story

The songs raised me. So we built a night around them.

Retro Wave started as a simple idea: play the tracks that actually shaped a generation, in a room full of people who feel the same way about them. What began in Kampala is now becoming a ticketed platform across three countries.

Where It Started

Kampala After Dark

Retro Wave began as a Kampala nightlife night, built around one question at the door: what's the anthem that made you? Every set since has been a mix of that answer — decade-spanning, unapologetically nostalgic, and loud in all the right places.

Four editions in, the format hasn't changed. What's changed is the reach — Retro Wave is now taking the same night to Nairobi and Kigali, with one platform handling tickets, history and discovery across all three.
