Where It Started

Entebbe, and one question at the door

Retro Wave began at Ark Gardens, Akright City in Entebbe on 18 April 2025, built around one question: what is the song that still stops you where you stand? Every set since has been an answer to it — decade-spanning, unapologetically nostalgic, and loud in all the right places.

Three editions in, the format has not changed and neither has the venue. What is changing is the reach: the same night is heading for Nairobi and Kigali, with one platform handling tickets, history and discovery across all three countries.

Questions

Good to know

Where are events held? Every edition so far has been at Ark Gardens, Akright City in Entebbe, Uganda. Nairobi and Kigali dates open as they are confirmed, and all of them appear on the events page.
How do I pay? MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or Visa and Mastercard. You approve mobile money on your own handset — there is nothing to install.
Can I pass my ticket to someone else? Yes. Tickets can be reassigned from your ticket link, no new purchase needed. Each QR is single-use, so a screenshot of someone else's ticket will not scan twice.
Is there an age limit? Retro Wave nights are 18+ and valid ID is required at the door.
